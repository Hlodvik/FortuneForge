# Slot bonus rounds

`SlotBonusRounds` is a pure queue and collected-wager kernel. Its caller supplies `CollectedBonusRules`: ordered tracks and seal mappings, completion targets, trusted native-point wager bounds, and registered math models with award lengths and permitted modes. The kernel has no game registry, RNG, payout calculation, account access or storage. Different rule instances do not share mutable progression.

Midnight Vault is the only title opted in. `MidnightVaultBonusRounds` supplies its frozen configuration and preserves the existing Midnight record and JSON shapes through thin adapters. Other titles retain their existing paths. The separately authorized Rainbow Realm collection-target change to 60 is independent of this extraction.

Each round stores its identifier, total spins, remaining spins, locked wager, feature mode and math-model ID. The first round is active; the remaining rounds are queued in order. Admission consumes only the head. A later trigger appends a new round without changing an unfinished round's wager, mode or version. Simultaneous triggers create separate rounds rather than one combined bank. Cash bets are rejected while any Midnight round remains.

Collection state stores exact gem counts and exact sums of the native-point wagers associated with those gems. Incoming gems fill a track to its configured target in order. The completed track's weighted average is rounded once to the nearest native point, with positive half points rounded up, and becomes that round's locked wager. Overflow gems retain their own wager contribution. No running-average truncation or previously rounded average is used to price the next round. Scatter awards use the triggering spin's wager.

Midnight's active model is `midnight-vault-paytable-v2`, with scalar 1.0 and integer native payout rates. J (`3`) pays 2× for five symbols; A (`5`) pays 7×. The other native rates remain unchanged. New scatter and completed-track awards each create a ten-spin round. The gem target remains 30. Fifteen active symbol identities and the J/Q/K/A reel weights remain intact, including the existing conditional POWER boost behavior. The staged gem-pile presentation is approximate and does not show an exact countdown; authoritative progression remains exact on the server.

Cash wagers retain their existing even-point menu from 2 through 2000 points, with each point worth R0.25. A server-derived weighted bonus wager may be an odd integer within those bounds. That entitlement does not make the odd amount an available cash bet. At scalar 1.0, integer native rates and the integer pattern award avoid wager-size-dependent per-match point rounding.

`midnight-vault-paytable-v1` remains available with its exact original rates and scalar 1.09. Its scatter and collection retriggers remain six and seven spins. An existing unversioned awarded bank migrates to one v1 carry round with its actual remaining spins, wager and mode; it is not rounded up to ten. Descendant awards retain their admitted model. Historical outcome replay uses frozen engine definitions and receipts, rather than today's active paytable.

The Firestore adapter persists the ordered `midnightBonusRounds` list and uses the free-spin currency counter only as total remaining bookkeeping. State and settlement responses expose the active round's remaining count and the queued rounds separately. Midnight state recovery reads the queue and balances consistently in one transaction.

Admission pins the selected head and saves before/after-consumption snapshots before deducting a spin or boost. Settlement checks that receipt and guard, pays the admitted outcome, advances exact collection state, appends new awards, and commits the resulting queue with the financial records. The private receipt contains `midnightBonusEvidence`: the admitted round, queue snapshots, exact collection counts and wager totals, and newly awarded rounds. Accepted participation evidence is written in the same settlement transaction.

Expired admissions with the new snapshots can be compensated atomically after the existing timeout. Recovery restores the consumed free spin and boost, creates deterministic refund records, and marks the old admission recovered before issuing another. A late settlement for that old admission is rejected. Pre-queue unfinished admissions without sufficient snapshots fail closed; the implementation does not invent a refund or claim universal crash recovery. Disconnect handling and idempotent financial settlement remain separate from the pure kernel.

The canonical `tools/FortuneForge.SlotMath` path for `--game dragon-hoard-v1` now uses the same Midnight FIFO helper and `SpinAdmitted`, carrying locked rounds, exact collection totals, pity and POWER balances. Other games keep the original loop and report paths; the source reconstruction proof is recorded with the integer-math maintenance evidence. The focused harness and independent oracle also exercise the shared queue lifecycle. Two independent 250,000-cycle holdouts measured 90.1404% and 90.0960%, identically at 2/100/2000 points, with approximate confidence intervals inside the 88–92% policy. Separate integer proofs cover all cash and derived wagers; paid emulator tests cover exact ledger settlement. Mixed-schedule confirmation retains the earlier short-sample outlier and supports the tested schedule, not every adaptive betting history. These are measured model/source results, not exact theoretical RTP, certification or final combined-release claims.

Money-path tests use isolated local Firestore-emulator users and actual admission/settlement transactions. They do not spend a real wallet, verify a deployed production environment, or establish every possible historical recovery case. Coupled-wager checks establish proportional native-point payouts under their declared cases; mixed collection wagers deliberately produce separately locked round averages.

Wukong may later receive a distinct bonus or pachinko-style feature. That remains a design idea only. This change implements no pachinko mechanics and does not opt Wukong or any additional title into the round kernel.

The ten-title reuse plan does not make Wukong an adopter of Midnight's new ten-spin format. Wukong retains its existing profile and engine path. Paw symbols with no cash symbols can pay zero; no payout floor or guaranteed reward is introduced. The current legacy cash-collection implementation doubles the cash sum for two or more paws, rather than multiplying by every paw above two. Reconciling that existing behavior with the separately stated per-paw product formula requires its own explicit change and historical math review; this Midnight change does not retune it.
