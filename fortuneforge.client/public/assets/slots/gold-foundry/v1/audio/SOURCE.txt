wooden door movement. The recordings were trimmed, equalized, layered,
and crossfaded for looping.

forge-bellows-and-crackling-fire.wav (current mix)
Mixed, trimmed, EQ'd, pitch-adjusted, and crossfaded into a 1.94-second
loop at 48 kHz. Two crank and bellows strokes are paired with recorded
flame bursts, forge crackle, and crackling branches. The earlier
forge-bellows-and-roaring-fire.wav uses the balloon burner in place of the
branching fire. forge-bellows-and-flare.wav and crank-bellows-and-flame.wav
use the wood boiler. The earliest mix also omits Gasoline fire #1.
