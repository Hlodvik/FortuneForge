# Pirates' Fortune sound sources

The following effects are sourced from OpenGameArt and used under Creative
Commons Zero (CC0). CC0 permits commercial use, adaptation, and distribution
without attribution. Attribution remains listed here for traceability.

- `pirate-helm-spin.ogg` — “Spinning Wheel” by themightyglider. Used as the
  looping wooden helm-crank during a spin.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/spinning-wheel-0
- `pirate-ship-theme-loop.ogg` — “Pirate Ship Theme Loop” by beardalaxy.
  Used as the looping background music.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/pirate-ship-theme-loop
- `pirate-small-win.wav` — Original two-note doubloon chime, generated for
  Fortune Forge. Used for ordinary wins.
- `pirate-seagull-miss.wav` — “Solo Seagull Sound Effects” by Rango Mango,
  `Seagull Ambient 6.wav`. Used for a larger winning outcome.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/solo-seagull-sound-effects
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/solo-seagull-sound-effects
- `pirate-seagull-win.wav` — “Solo Seagull Sound Effects” by Rango Mango,
  `Seagull Ambient 2.wav`. Used for ordinary winning outcomes.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/solo-seagull-sound-effects
- `pirate-wave-ambience.flac` — “Beach / Ocean Waves” by jasinski,
  `wave_02_cc0-18363__jasinski__alkaibeach.flac`. Used as the no-win result.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves
Source pages and their stated CC0 terms were checked on 2026-08-27.
