# Midnight Vault audio

Normal and bonus wins use `public/assets/slots/midnight-vault/v4/audio/vault-win-noir.wav`, a 3.2-second excerpt (156.849–160.049 seconds) of Kevin MacLeod’s [**I Knew a Guy**](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?Search=Search&isrc=USUAN1100199). The excerpt adds short fades and a gain adjustment. The track and excerpt are credited under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) in the game and its audio credits.

The unmodified background recording plays at 0.16 base gain and follows saved mute and volume settings.

The separate GRAN DARTS brass audition and its source note are retained in the local preview workspace for review only.

Midnight buttons use the existing CC0 Kenney Casino Audio `chip-lay-1.ogg` at base gain0.11. This effect obeys mute, volume and results-only settings; the main spin button retains its lever cue without an added click. No other title opts into the button event.
