Gather ingredients

Six ingredients can land anywhere on the reels. Each one is a different way to brew a potion.

Feed the cauldron

Landed ingredients stay in the cauldron until you brew. The leading ingredient gives the potion its color.

Land the Grand Grimoire

Land this symbol to light the Grimoire on its lectern. Open the Tome and activate a page before you spin again.

Choose a Grimoire boost

Turn the pages to pick a pair. Each featured ingredient gives two units per landing for your next five paid spins. The Grand Grimoire stays off the reels until the boost ends.

Bottle a potion

Brew at 8 units total or 5 of one ingredient. For most potions, the most common ingredient sets the effect and the others add potency. A rare brew uses two units each of four different ingredients, including Mandrake Root. Each bottle’s locked wager is the average of the paid wagers that produced its ingredients, rounded down to an available wager.

Put your potion to work

Bottles appear below the cauldron. Hover, focus, or press and hold a bottle to see its effect. Click or tap the bottle to use it. Only one potion can be active at a time. Its locked wager applies to your next three paid spins, then your previous wager selection returns.

The eight potions

Ordinary potions draw their effect from the main ingredient and potency from the others. Rare potions follow their own recipes. Payout examples use an R3 locked wager and base-strength potions.

Wolfsbane Wine & Wolfsbane Cordial

As the main ingredient, Wolfsbane Wine brews a cordial that boosts VII and pentagram payline payouts.

As a secondary ingredient, it adds 1 potency per unit.

Mandrake Root & Mandrake Tonic

As the main ingredient, Mandrake Root brews a tonic that boosts Scribe Mark payouts by 20–23% based on potency. The minimum bonus ranges from R0.25 to R1.25 based on the wager.

As a secondary ingredient, it adds 1 potency per unit.

Nightshade Berries & Nightshade Elixir

As the main ingredient, Nightshade Berries brew an elixir that makes constellations land more often.

As a secondary ingredient, it adds 2 potency per unit.

Mooncap Mushroom & Mooncap Draught

As the main ingredient, Mooncap Mushroom brews a draught that makes VII and pentagrams land more often.

As a secondary ingredient, it adds 2 potency per unit.

Phoenix Feather & Phoenix Infusion

As the main ingredient, Phoenix Feather brews an infusion that makes Enchanted Wands land more often.

As a secondary ingredient, it adds 3 potency per unit.

Quicksilver & Quicksilver Essence

As the main ingredient, Quicksilver brews an essence that makes wand-collected constellations pay more.

As a secondary ingredient, it adds 3 potency per unit.

Astral Convergence

Wands and constellations land more often. Constellations collected by a wand pay more.

Rare brew. Use two units each of Mandrake Root, Quicksilver, and two other ingredients.

Runebound Elixir

VII and pentagrams land more often. Every winning payline pays more.

Rare brew. Use two units of Mandrake Root and two units each of three other ingredients from Nightshade Berries, Mooncap Mushroom, Phoenix Feather, and Wolfsbane Wine.

Cast with a constellation

Land a wand and a constellation anywhere on the same spin. One wand pays out every visible constellation.

Payline wins
