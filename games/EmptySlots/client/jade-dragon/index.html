Jade Dragon uses Fortune Forge’s EmptySlots engine. This is a 5×4 grid with 23 paylines. Collection features expand to six rows. Only the server evaluates wins and settles your balance.

Three Free Games Pearls award six spins. Wilds pay native runs of three or five, and substitute in runs of five. Runs may begin anywhere on a payline. The engine uses a payout scale of 0.866 and integer-point rounding; the table shows native backend multipliers, not guaranteed currency returns.

Collect 22 seals on any track to unlock eight Dragon’s Awakening spins: one reel is copied to another, the board expands by two rows, and two wilds are injected where eligible. Free-game wagers stay locked to their server entitlement.

Jade Trident symbols can award a three-symbol straight-pattern payout. Dragon Sparks award special points on exact matches of three or five; spending eight sparks activates the backend boost. The meter, collector, and value symbols are currently inactive.

Each point is worth R0.25. New paid spins in this presentation are capped at R100. A previously awarded server bonus uses its original wager. Auto stops when disabled, on an error, when rules open, or when the tab is hidden. A submitted round is settled by the server even if its animation is interrupted.

The prototype’s cascades, three-way bonus selection, and pick-a-pearl gift are not rules of this backend. No client-generated credits or payouts are used here.
