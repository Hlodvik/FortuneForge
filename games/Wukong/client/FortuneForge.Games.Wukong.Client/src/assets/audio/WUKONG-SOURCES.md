# Wukong sound sources

The Wukong effects in this directory are original procedural sound designs generated for FortuneForge by `tools/generate_wukong_audio.py`. They use additive synthesis and deterministic filtered noise; no third-party samples are included.

- `wukong-staff-whoosh.wav`: spin-control staff sweep
- `wukong-cloud-rush.wav`: seamless reel-motion cloud bed
- `wukong-woodblock.wav`: reel landing
- `wukong-soft-step.wav`: restrained losing-spin cue
- `wukong-temple-bell.wav`: ordinary win
- `wukong-gong-win.wav`: premium/bonus win
- `wukong-celestial-chime.wav`: five-symbol celebration
